# Selected arrangement

`selected-nicest.png` is Arrangement 02.

It is the strongest architectural compromise in the final batch: 74.1 overall score, 67% net fill, 80% circulation, an 82% coherent 60-degree orientation field, 78% controlled novelty, and 88% module reuse. The core is legible, the yellow circulation forms a connected branching spine, and the room field follows the site's rotated geometry without reverting to a world-axis grid.

Arrangement 01 has the highest numeric score (75.0) and fill (74%), but it is visually more conventional and orthogonal. Arrangement 06 is the most formally adventurous, including triangular tip modules, but its fill and circulation are weaker.
